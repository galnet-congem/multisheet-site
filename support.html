Before you write in

A few things that help us help you faster:

  • What you were trying to do (e.g. "Delete 3 sheets")
  • What happened instead, including any error message
  • A link to the spreadsheet, if you're able to share it

Common questions

Does MultiSheet see my data?

No. MultiSheet only manages sheet tabs (names, order, visibility) — it does not read or store the contents of your cells. See our Privacy Policy for details.

I deleted a sheet by mistake — can I get it back?

Use the Undo button in the MultiSheet sidebar right after the action. Undo supports multiple recent actions, not just the last one.

A download didn't work

Try again after refreshing the sidebar. If it still fails, email us with the sheet name and the format you were exporting (PDF, CSV, or XLSX).
