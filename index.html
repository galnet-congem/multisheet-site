MultiSheet

Bulk-manage the tabs in your Google Sheets, right from a sidebar.

MultiSheet is a Google Sheets add-on for working with many sheet tabs at once, instead of clicking through them one at a time.

What it does

  • Hide, unhide, or isolate sheets (Hide Others / Show All)
  • Reorder sheets — move to start, end, left, or right
  • Sort selected sheets alphabetically (A–Z or Z–A)
  • Add a prefix or suffix to sheet names, or rename them sequentially
  • Duplicate selected sheets
  • Download selected sheets as PDF, CSV, or XLSX
  • Delete selected sheets, with multi-step Undo for every action

What it doesn't do

MultiSheet only manages sheet tabs — their names, order, and visibility. It does not read, edit, or store the data inside your cells, and it does not send your spreadsheet content to any external server.

Get MultiSheet

MultiSheet is available as a free add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace.
