Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what MultiSheet ("the add-on") does and does not do with your data when you install and use it in Google Sheets.

What MultiSheet accesses

MultiSheet runs entirely inside your own Google account using Google Apps Script. To do its job, it requests:

  • Google Sheets access — to read the list of sheet tabs in your active spreadsheet, and to hide, reorder, rename, duplicate, or delete tabs you select.
  • Limited Google Drive access — only to create a temporary backup copy of a sheet so that "Undo" can work after a delete, and to generate a temporary file when you use "Download Selected." These temporary files are deleted automatically once they are no longer needed.
  • A connection to Google's own Sheets export service — used only to generate the PDF, CSV, or XLSX file when you choose to download sheets. This connection goes to Google's own docs.google.com service, not to any third party.

What MultiSheet does not do

  • It does not read, analyze, or store the data inside your spreadsheet cells.
  • It does not send your spreadsheet content to MultiSheet Labs or to any third party.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.
  • It does not require you to create a separate account — access is authorized entirely through your existing Google account.

Where data is stored

MultiSheet keeps a small amount of temporary state (so that "Undo" and "Refresh" work correctly) using Google's own Apps Script storage, scoped to the specific spreadsheet you're working in. This state never leaves Google's infrastructure and is not accessible to MultiSheet Labs.

Data sharing

MultiSheet Labs does not sell, rent, or share any data accessed by this add-on with third parties.
